Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Supper lets kitchens become friends and share the recipes they love. It only works if it stays a friendly place to swap food, so these rules apply to everything you share with friends or through a share link.

Share recipes, kindly

  • Share recipes and cooking — not anything else.
  • No hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, violent, or otherwise offensive content in titles, descriptions, photos or notes.
  • No spam, advertising, or impersonating other people or kitchens.
  • Credit creators. Recipes saved from someone else's site or video keep a link to the original, and Supper sends friends there for the full method.

You're in control

  • Friends only see what your kitchen chooses to share (nothing, favourites, loved recipes, or everything you've made), and you can hide any single recipe.
  • Friends never see your notes, cooking history, or member emails.
  • You can remove a friend or block a kitchen at any time. Blocked kitchens can't send you requests and aren't told.
  • Use Report recipe (••• on any friend's recipe) to flag anything that breaks these rules.

What happens when something's reported

We review every report within 24 hours. Content that breaks these guidelines is removed, and kitchens or accounts that post it may be removed from Supper.
