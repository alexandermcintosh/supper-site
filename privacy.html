Supper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Supper is a shared household cookbook app. This policy describes what data the app handles and how.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored hashed), used only to sign you in.
  • Your content: recipes you save (links, page text and photos you capture), cover photos, cook-log entries and notes, chat messages with the in-app cooking assistant, and household membership. Content is shared with the other members of your household — that's the point of the app.
  • Tastes: optional names, diets, spice preferences, dislikes and food allergies you enter for household members, used only to tailor suggestions. Allergy information is health-related; enter only what you're comfortable storing, and remove it any time.

How it's processed

  • Data is stored with Supabase (hosted in Canada).
  • Captured recipe content (page text, photos of recipes), your household's tastes, and chat messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to transcribe recipes, suggest dishes, and power the cooking assistant. The app asks for your permission before any content is shared with this AI service, and you can turn AI features off at any time. Anthropic's API does not train on this data by default.

Shared recipe links

Recipes are private to your household. If you tap Share on a recipe, Supper creates a public link that anyone with the link can view (the recipe, its cover photo, and your household's name). For recipes saved from another website or video, shared pages show the ingredients and link to the original creator for the method.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics trackers, no sale or sharing of your data with anyone else.
  • No access to your contacts, location, or anything beyond what you explicitly capture into the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account any time in the app (Add tab → Delete account). If you're the last member of your household, the household and all its recipes and share links are permanently deleted with it.
