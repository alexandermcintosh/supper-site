Support

Supper is a shared household cookbook: save recipes from anywhere, decide what to cook, and keep track of what you've made.

How do I save a recipe from TikTok, YouTube, or a website?

Tap Share in that app, then choose Save Recipe. If you don't see it, scroll the share sheet's app row to the end, tap More, and enable it.

How do I share a library with my partner or family?

On the Add tab you'll find your household's invite code. The other person creates an account and enters the code to join your library.

How do I tell Supper about allergies or dislikes?

Add tab → Our tastes. Allergies are always kept out of suggestions; dislikes and diets are respected unless you switch that off in Decide → Tune.

A recipe came in incomplete.

Some videos don't include the recipe in their caption. Those land under Needs review — tap one to fill in the gaps or retry.

How do I delete my account?

Add tab → scroll to the bottom → Delete account.
